> *"The only trading platform that profits from you not losing money."*

---

## 🏛️ Invariants Pinned to AGENTS.md
1. **Never show a result without its context** (drawdown, sample size, buy-and-hold comparison).
2. **Never let marketing outrun the data** (claims computed from DB, never hand-written).
3. **Losses get equal billing to wins, everywhere**.
4. **Tamper-evident ledger integrity** (SHA-256 hash chaining, published snapshots).
5. **No casino incentives**: Features that encourage overtrading are forbidden.
